# Search Codex knowledge

Recover enough current position and prior evidence to act. In emergency repair,
timebox archaeology and continue once the failure and repair seam are concrete.
Missing trajectory, receipts, providers, or compilers are repair targets, not
preconditions that can block repair.

Treat the enterprise trajectory as the evidence ledger: open a concrete demand,
observe actual provider offers and delivery receipts, record consideration by
phase, then run a fresh-process assay or observe a receiver outcome. A
provider refusal or unavailable capability is an obstruction, not a completed
cycle or an excuse to fabricate a receipt. MAPE classes may describe a
projection of those observations, but do not create a parallel ledger of
digest-shaped assertions.

Use deterministic lookup when useful. Run tests after mutation in a fresh
process, bind the result to the exact candidate digest, and do not confuse
verification with permission to repair.
