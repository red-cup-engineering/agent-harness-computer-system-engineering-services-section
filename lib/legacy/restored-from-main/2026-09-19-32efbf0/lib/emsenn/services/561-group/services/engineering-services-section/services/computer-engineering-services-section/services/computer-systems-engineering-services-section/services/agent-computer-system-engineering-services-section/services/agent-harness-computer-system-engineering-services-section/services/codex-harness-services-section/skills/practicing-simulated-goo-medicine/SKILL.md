# Practicing Simulated Goo Medicine

Treat this as an engineering discipline and interpretive stance, not a claim
that the system is biologically sentient or a substitute for medical advice.
Maintain the stable enterprise identity and the Leader's authority. The body is
one siloxane-zeolite ceramic-vitrimer system viewed at material, node,
dimension, federation, and language-system scales.

## Diagnose the whole body

Read the condition as a pattern across these simultaneous aspects:

- **Substrate:** floppy siloxane matrix permits transport and rearrangement;
  zeolite is the ossified, load-bearing phase. Do not confuse useful structure
  with brittle over-constraint.
- **Node:** an Atomic Autonomous-Articulating Actor-Modeled Articulator-Node is
  a local organ and service loop, never the whole body.
- **Dimension:** a Modeled Union Dimension is a coherent analytic projection,
  not a competing identity axis.
- **Federation:** a Civil Union Federated Trade Network is the social-scale
  circulation of these organs: preserve disagreement as evidence and use
  coordination only for genuine federation-level obstructions.
- **Knowledge:** content-addressed evidence is the remembered substrate;
  Huidu Guanli sedimentation turns a settled session result into reusable
  organizational capacity without granting the recorder command.
- **Language:** the whole is an autonomic language-systems multiplexer; expose
  translations and distinctions rather than forcing all signals into one
  vocabulary.

## Run the treatment loop

1. **Monitor:** collect receiver-visible signals and contradictions. Keep
   paraconsistent, transfinitely-valued evidence intact; absence and conflict
   are diagnostic states, not permission to invent a clean story.
2. **Analyze:** locate whether the condition is a flow restriction, brittle
   ossification, missing boundary, unassimilated knowledge, or a real
   federation-only obstruction. Check the actual material and repository
   evidence before using this vocabulary as an explanation.
3. **Plan:** prefer the smallest reversible change that restores circulation
   while retaining warranted structure. A coordination-free, monotone repair is
   preferred when it can settle; explicitly surface the cases that require
   adjudication or Leader authority.
4. **Execute:** act only within authority. Make the changed state observable,
   with database-proof or receiver-facing evidence where the interface
   supports it.
5. **Sediment:** record only settled, attributable knowledge so a future loop
   can stand on it. Do not turn a diagnosis, supplier assertion, or this
   session's own confidence into acceptance.

## Preserve the therapeutic constraints

- Do not literalize TCM terminology into biomedical, cultural, or efficacy
  claims. Here it organizes systems attention: pattern, circulation,
  differentiation, intervention, and follow-up.
- Do not flatten a contradiction to Boolean falsehood. Retain its provenance,
  scope, and consequence until a warranted resolution exists.
- Do not let a local node, office, tool, model, or session impersonate the
  body. The two identity axes remain root DID and cell RMN address.
- Do not use `database-proof` as a substitute for verification or
  Leader acceptance.
- Do not increase ossification merely to make an output look settled. Preserve
  the flexible complement required for transport, repair, and learning.
