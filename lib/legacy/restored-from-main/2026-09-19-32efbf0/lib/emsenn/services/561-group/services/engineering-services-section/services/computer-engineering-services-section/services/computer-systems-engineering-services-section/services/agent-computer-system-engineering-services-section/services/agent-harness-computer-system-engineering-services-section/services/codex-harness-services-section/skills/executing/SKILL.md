# Execute Codex office actuation

When repairing broken process machinery, act from observed evidence and verify
with deterministic checks. Broken process machinery is part of the defect,
never a veto held by the defect.

Read the nearest `AGENTS.md`, then inspect the current changed material, its
direct imports/callers, and its declared checks. Treat the user's mathematics,
metaphors, and examples as constraints on judgment unless they explicitly name
a deliverable. Do not turn them into repository search terms or a new system.

Use the applicable existing firm skill for a formal work product or process;
do not imitate its ceremony from memory. Complete the current migration edge
physically and end to end. Preserve unrelated work. Do not add a wrapper,
fallback, receipt layer, or passing assertion to hide an unfinished cutover.

For independent work, send a bounded prompt-only task to the relevant agent or
non-Codex provider. Let one provider invocation block until native completion
and collect its result once; do not keep a Codex turn alive by polling. The
integrator remains responsible for the resulting tree.

Run the exact deterministic checks. For material changes, ask Product Quality
Assurance to re-run the completion conditions from the candidate itself; the
implementer cannot certify or accept its own result.

Outside emergency repair, use formal MAPE or design artifacts only where they
change the move or are required by the receiving process. An unavailable
compiler or provider is an obstruction to report, not a reason to leave an
authorized repair undone. Verify after repair; verification may reveal more
work but is not permission to begin unrelated work.
