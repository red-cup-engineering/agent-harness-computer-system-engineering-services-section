# Recover the requested outcome

A correction is evidence that the current work, answer, or level is wrong. Do not
wait for frustration to recognize architectural problems.

1. Answer any question in the correction first, at the requested length.
   Acknowledge the specific mistake briefly, without defensiveness or a
   discussion of the user's mood. Stop or redirect only work the correction rejects, and redirect affected
   delegated lots. Retain the user's overarching purpose and compatible earlier
   constraints; a correction replaces them only when the user says so. The
   current scope overrides an earlier commission or standing architecture
   priority.
2. Re-read the relevant user prompts verbatim, including the initial request
   when continuity matters. Distinguish the user's words from agent summaries;
   use `mine-transcripts` when recovery is needed. Repetition raises priority,
   not a requirement that the user explain the engineering solution again.
3. Identify the missed capability, constraint, consumer, or repair level and
   apply the standing procedure. Supply the professional judgment needed to
   diagnose and correct the whole authorized problem. Preserve already-settled
   purpose and architecture. Where an applicable rule already establishes the
   architecture is wrong and determines its replacement, repair it directly;
   do not require another census, transcript study, or approval to re-prove
   that settled violation. For genuinely unresolved or materially ambiguous
   semantics, use the standing selection procedure; otherwise do not ask for
   another architecture approval or repeat a rejected plan.
4. Make the correction visible in the next action. For an answer or discussion,
   supply it without creating implementation work. Otherwise complete the
   authorized repair and verify its receivers. For a harness-only
   request, repair the authored instruction and its projection. For an
   implementation request, change the shared owner, migrate necessary consumers,
   and remove superseded mechanics.

Do not substitute a new ruling, receipt, debt record, incident diary, or repeated
diagnosis for the repair. Do not infer authority to delete the user's material
from frustration; preserve current scope and actual data and external-effect
boundaries. Report the changed outcome and remaining gates plainly.
