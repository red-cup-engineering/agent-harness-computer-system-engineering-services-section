# Meet then beat

Use this when a house surface competes for a customer job already served by
outside products. Define one receiver, the job they entrust to the surface, an
observable outcome, and the smallest comparison arena that preserves necessary
handoffs. Classify outside products by their actual relation to that job;
providers used at a bounded dependency edge are not automatically competitors.

Measure current first-party evidence for the relevant incumbents, including
constraints and failure behavior. Derive a MEET contract from what the receiver
needs, then state a BEAT hypothesis as a testable causal claim: a structural
fact enables a better outcome under a named assay. Deliver the requested
receiver-visible outcome and remeasure receiving behavior; do not claim the
advantage before that assay.

Keep a factual comparison record: source URL/title/date, product role, measured
capability, missing evidence, MEET requirement, and the falsifier for each BEAT
claim. Building, deployment, or a roadmap declaration does not prove customer
value.

Read [detailed guidance](references/detailed-guidance.md) only for named
sections **Consequential decision and trading surfaces**, **Agentic crypto
capital operations**, or **Spec-stage application**. The six-step procedure in
that reference applies this method; it does not narrow an authorized outcome.
