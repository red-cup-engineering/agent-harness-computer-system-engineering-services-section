its contents be captured into distributed datastores first. House properties never become captive feature
feeders for a replaceable competitor. A provider the house deliberately uses
for custody, execution, identity, payment, or another bounded capability is
not thereby the incumbent for the house's whole product. Govern that
dependency at its actual boundary; compare the house surface with products
that perform the same customer job.

## The procedure

### 1. Bound the arena and the receiver

Write one sentence naming the receiver, the job they entrust to the surface,
the outcome they can observe, and the boundary of the comparison. Begin with
a real waiting customer when one exists. "Trading software" or "all software
we use" is not one arena: decompose the customer journey into the smallest
capability arenas that can be compared and shipped, then preserve their
end-to-end handoffs.

For each arena classify every outside product before comparing it:

- **same-job incumbent** — a product the receiver could choose instead;
- **upstream provider or venue** — a bounded dependency the house uses;
- **complement** — a tool used before or after the house surface; or
- **reference implementation** — evidence about how a capability can work.

One company can occupy more than one role. Do not compare a house customer
experience to an upstream API as though they were substitutes, and do not
pretend a governed dependency has already been replaced.

### 2. Select an incumbent portfolio

Name explicit products, not only categories. For each arena choose the fewest
incumbents that cover the receiver's real alternatives and the relevant
capability classes. A multi-class surface therefore has a portfolio, not one
ceremonial rival. State why each product was included and which class remains
unmeasured. An unnamed or misclassified incumbent is an unmeasured one.

### 3. Measure current evidence, including failure behavior

Measure each incumbent from first-party external evidence: its current docs,
program disclosures, agreements or terms, fee schedules, status surface, and
runnable product when access permits. A house note, search-result snippet,
email paraphrase, model memory, or third-party review is a discovery lead, not
evidence for a current product claim. An email may establish that a provider
announced a change; follow its links and assay the governing first-party
surface before deriving a customer decision.

A market's current size is a fact to record, never by itself a reason to
deprioritize building a surface (owner-ruled 2026-09-05). Measure the incumbent's
present volume or the arena's present capitalization and record it dated, then
size the opportunity against the house forward thesis rather than the snapshot
alone: the founder's standing belief is that agentic commerce is early, so a thin
current number (an agent-payment rail clearing tens of thousands of dollars a
day, a micro-SKU with a handful of settlements) bounds today's revenue, not
tomorrow's, and is a sizing input, never a verdict against the build. State both
numbers, what the arena clears now and what the thesis expects of its trajectory,
and let the build decision rest on the mechanism's merit under that thesis. The
companion capital rule is the fintech-friday term's: an empty account never
filters what the org develops.

For conflicting first-party claims, prefer the source that is both most
authoritative for the claim and applicable to the receiver: executed account
agreement or program-specific legal disclosure; then current fee/rate schedule
and operational notice; then support/API documentation; then product page or
marketing copy. Record the conflict rather than silently choosing whichever
claim is more favorable. Jurisdiction, account type, asset, enrollment cohort,
and program version can make two apparently conflicting claims simultaneously
true.

Do not collapse dates. Every evidence row records the date the source says it
was published or last updated when available, the date a rule or rate became
or will become effective when stated, and the UTC date-time it was observed.
An undated page has `published_date: unknown`; a source without an effective
date has `effective_date: unknown`. The observation date proves only when the
source was retrieved. Never infer current applicability merely because a URL
still resolves. If live retrieval is unavailable, label the public-knowledge
snapshot and owe remeasurement before the spec ships.

Keep the result as derived, updateable data in the owning section, renderable
by ops.561.group. Each atomic assertion needs:

- arena, product, product role, receiver and jurisdiction/account applicability;
- capability or constraint, normalized value, unit, and exact source language;
- canonical source URL or runnable surface, source title, disclosure class,
  published/updated date, effective date, and observed-at date-time;
- evidence kind (`documented`, `observed`, or `assayed`), assay account mode
  (`public`, `paper`, `sandbox`, or `live`), result, confidence, and conflicts;
  and
- `unknown_reason`, `next_evidence`, and owner whenever a required value is not
  established.

Use `unknown`, never `none`, `zero`, or an invented default, for an unestablished
fact. Distinguish `not offered`, `not applicable`, `not disclosed`, `not
observed`, `not assayed`, and `inaccessible`; each has a different next action.
Marketing copy proves a documented claim, not working behavior. A missing UI
control does not prove a capability is unavailable, and an API schema does not
prove that the receiver's account is eligible. Measure consequential failure
behavior too: reversibility, degraded modes, custody, fees, support, data
export, auditability, and what the product simulates or omits.

Paper, sandbox, and public-demo evidence must carry an omission ledger. At
minimum check whether the environment reproduces eligibility and enrollment,
inventory or asset selection, actual borrow demand and allocation, changing
rates and revenue share, accrual and payment timing, tax lots or reporting,
collateral and counterparty mechanics, voting/proxy consequences, recall and
sale interaction, statements, provider notices, suspension, opt-out, and
close-out. Mark each `simulated`, `stubbed`, `omitted`, or `unknown`, with the
source or observation supporting that classification. Paper buying power,
fills, or portfolio returns never imply that a securities-lending program was
simulated.

### 4. Derive the MEET contract

For every capability the receiver actually needs, record:

- the incumbent baseline and evidence;
- our present evidence (`absent`, `source-only`, `tested`, `deployed`, or
  `receiver-accepted`);
- the smallest end-to-end acceptance assay; and
- the owning capability and next gap.

The derived gaps are the arena's engineering roadmap. Parity is not a checkbox
for having similarly named code: the receiver must be able to complete the
same job, including onboarding, authorization, normal operation, explanation,
failure recovery, exit, and support. Legal, safety, accessibility, privacy,
and provider-contract obligations are part of competence, not post-launch
garnish.

### 5. Form and try to falsify the BEAT hypothesis

State the proposed advantage as a receiver-visible, measurable hypothesis:
**because of structural fact X, the house can produce outcome Y under assay Z,
while the named incumbents cannot without contradicting X**. Candidate house
strengths include provenance, agent-and-human symmetry, cross-property
composition, MUD-native operation, and the internal market, but none is a
differentiator merely because it exists in our vocabulary.

Check every named incumbent's measured surface for the same capability,
including beta and differently named forms. Then actively search for a
counterexample to structural fact X. If an incumbent already ships it, it is a
MEET gap. If X is not demonstrated, retain it as a research hypothesis. Do not
claim a performance advantage from architecture, theory, a backtest, or a
single successful run.

### 6. Ship one receiver-visible wedge and remeasure

Choose the smallest gap closure that completes a real handoff in the customer
journey and makes the beat hypothesis more testable. Assay the exact surface
the receiver will use; record whether the result is local, deployed, observed,
or accepted. A closed MEET gap, a falsified or strengthened BEAT hypothesis,
or a shipped differentiator is roadmap-visible work. A surface with no named
incumbent, no receiver assay, and no defensible hypothesis faces the foundry's
kill question.

Remeasure at material incumbent releases, provider-contract changes, before
deployment, and on a bounded cadence after release. A provider email about a
new economic or custody program is such a trigger, not merely product news.

## Consequential decision and trading surfaces

When the surface can move money, incur tax, alter rights, or act for a customer,
MEET includes the whole authority-and-evidence loop:

`mandate -> data as-of/provenance -> strategy version -> risk-sized intent ->
authorization -> venue instruction -> acknowledgement/fill -> reconciliation ->
customer explanation -> pause/revoke/exit`

Research richness increases candidate signal density; it does not waive this
loop. Keep scientific claims, strategy definitions, execution quality, and
customer outcomes separately assayable. Advance decision policies through
historical/out-of-sample evaluation, realistic simulation, paper or shadow
operation, bounded live stakes, and only then an expanded mandate. At every
stage publish the known simulation omissions, falsifier, risk envelope, stop
conditions, and rollback or revocation path. Never use future information,
silently change the strategy under evaluation, or promote corrected null
results into universal market claims.

Capital size never gates building this capacity (founder-ruled 2026-09-04:
"we need the capital trading capacity for when there is capital"). The loop
above is built and proven on paper and on small real positions now; a survey
of capital floors is a sizing input for the mandate, never a reason to defer
the tooling. Carrier: `561-group-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/fintech-friday.md`.

### Agentic crypto capital operations

A crypto-capital surface is not one arena and a protocol APY is not its
incumbent. Decompose the principal's journey into linked arenas before choosing
rivals:

- wallet discovery, custody, recovery, signing, and delegated authority;
- cross-wallet and cross-chain position observation, valuation, provenance,
  and accounting;
- research, belief capture, strategy definition, backtest/simulation, and
  strategy selection;
- transaction construction, routing, simulation, policy validation, signing,
  broadcast, finality, and reconciliation;
- position monitoring, reward harvesting, rebalancing, liquidation defense,
  allowance revocation, and orderly or emergency exit; and
- any public signal, recommendation, automation, or managed-capital service
  derived from the private operator.

Preserve the handoffs among these arenas. A read-only treasury observer can
MEET an analytics product while still leaving execution absent; a fixed-fork
exit simulation can MEET a scenario tool while deliberately refusing to emit
calldata; a signer or protocol can be an upstream provider without being the
same-job incumbent for the principal's control plane. Do not credit one arena's
evidence to another.

Choose and freshly measure an incumbent portfolio across the classes actually
in scope. Candidate classes include exchange-bot platforms; on-chain analytics
and yield aggregators; DeFi position automation and vault managers; smart
accounts, multisigs, delegated/session-key wallets, and agent-wallet SDKs;
transaction simulators and approval/revoke tools; portfolio, tax, and
reconciliation systems; and the protocols, bridges, routers, games, or other
venues competing for allocation. Products named in house research are
discovery leads, not current measurements. Protocols and games compete as
venues for capital, attention, or play even when they are upstream providers
to the house surface; compare their economic programs at that boundary rather
than pretending the protocol UI substitutes for the whole operator.

For each wallet and position, build an address-only, secret-free position graph
before optimizing a leg. Nodes include wallet or smart account, chain, asset,
approval, protocol contract and version, collateral, debt, LP or vault share,
reward entitlement, hedge or offset, bridge dependency, and tax/accounting lot;
edges include custody, control, conversion, liquidation, claim, correlation,
and required exit order. The graph is incomplete until inaccessible wallets,
unchecked chains, unknown contracts, stale prices, and missing exits are
represented as unknowns rather than zeros. Never expose a mnemonic, private
key, raw signature, or secret value to the model or competitive evidence
store. Secret availability proves neither recoverability nor safe autonomous
authority.

Measure strategies and venues on the principal's net outcome and changed risk,
not headline APR, reward emission, points, or a successful transaction. At
minimum include capital-at-risk and probability or conditions of ruin; realized
and unrealized return; gas, priority fees, price impact, slippage, routing and
claim costs; reward dilution and token-price dependence; borrow rate, collateral
factor, health factor and liquidation path; LP impermanent loss and range risk;
depeg, oracle, contract, upgrade/admin-key, governance, bridge, counterparty,
and chain-finality risk; withdrawal latency or queue; MEV/sandwich exposure;
tax/accounting consequences; monitoring burden; and the executable unwind at
the proposed size. Assay coupled strategies as one graph, including reward
farms, offsetting holdings, recursive lending, cross-protocol hedges, and game
economies. A profitable leg does not establish a profitable strategy when a
coupled leg, claim, rebalance, or exit is missing.

Represent a strategy as an executable game rule, not a theme or attractive
chart. State the arena and time horizon; observable state and provenance;
legal actions and forbidden moves; opponents, adversaries, and provider rule
changes; payoff including all costs and liabilities; resource and information
constraints; sizing rule; entry, hold, harvest, rebalance, and exit conditions;
falsifier; and version. Mathematical research, game semantics, topology, or
logic contributes only when translated into one of these typed elements and
compared with a simpler baseline. A principal's "vibe" or belief can be a
timestamped, provenance-bearing signal with a stated refuter; it is neither
reusable proof nor a predictive edge until out-of-sample evidence says so.

MEET for an autonomous own-wallet operator requires a deterministic authority
envelope outside the model. Name the human principal and encode wallet, chain,
asset, protocol, contract/router, action, per-transaction spend, cumulative
spend, gas, slippage, leverage, drawdown, loss, and time limits; allowable
approvals and allowance ceilings; first-use and manual-only actions; strategy
version; expiry; pause/revoke/kill path; and the independent signer that
enforces them. Keep observation, recommendation, transaction construction,
policy approval, signing, broadcast, and reconciliation as distinct evidence
states. The model must not sign or gain authority merely because secret words
exist in an environment. First-time or unknown contracts, unlimited approvals,
bridges, leverage, liquidation-sensitive borrowing, and mandate expansion stay
human-gated unless the principal has explicitly armed that exact class.

Treat a deliberate ruin-tolerant or moonshot sleeve as a bounded mandate, not
an exception to competence. Record its initial capital, maximum additional
funding, total-loss meaning, replenishment rule, maximum independent attempts,
cross-sleeve contagion barriers, and stop condition. Kelly sizing, expected
value, or simulation can inform the envelope but cannot authorize it. Compare
the aggressive sleeve against cash/hold, passive supply, simple LP or vault,
and the best measured incumbent automation at the same size and horizon. The
BEAT claim may be radically ambitious; the live authority granted to test it
must remain finitely inspectable and revocable.

The end-to-end live assay is chain-specific and receipt-complete:

`address-only discovery -> checked position graph -> as-of valuation ->
versioned strategy -> deterministic risk-sized intent -> fork or transaction
simulation -> human mandate check -> policy-enforced construction -> signer
outside the model -> broadcast -> receipt/finality -> balance and allowance
reconciliation -> explanation -> monitor/harvest/rebalance or unwind ->
pause/revoke/exit`

Paper returns, fork success, source code, RPC reads, a protocol dashboard, and
a transaction hash each prove only their own stage. Before increasing the
mandate, prove the exact chain, account, contract, amount, and postconditions;
assay partial fill or revert, stale oracle or RPC, gas spike, nonce conflict,
reorg/finality delay, reward-token collapse, allowance drain, protocol pause or
upgrade, liquidation approach, and unavailable exit. Public service work adds
the receiver's informed-consent, custody, suitability, marketing, sanctions,
tax/reporting, and jurisdictional boundaries; success on the principal's own
wallet does not establish competence to advise, custody, or execute for others.

Treat opt-in economic programs such as lending, yield, margin, or staking as a
customer decision surface of their own. Before recommending, enabling, or
automating one, compare and assay at least: enrollment and revocation; asset
scope and selection control; gross economics and every party's share; tax and
cashflow treatment; loss of voting or other rights; insurance/protection and
collateral custody; liquidity, recall, and close-out behavior; interaction with
the strategy and risk model; statement/reconciliation events; and informed
customer consent. Additional yield is not a BEAT result until net benefit and
changed risk are both visible to the receiver.

For an opt-in securities-lending trigger, do not limit the portfolio to firms
that advertise lending. Include the execution/custody provider at its actual
boundary, same-job managed-investing alternatives, strategy-authoring or
automation products, and research/backtest/execution platforms that frame the
receiver's other choices. For every selected product, create a `product_role`
row and a `securities_lending_capability` row. A product for which lending is
irrelevant or unavailable still gets a row with evidence-backed `not
applicable` or `not offered`; silence gets `not disclosed`, never a guessed
negative. This keeps provider economics distinct from competitor experience
while exposing where paper strategy products omit the changed custody and
rights state entirely.

The program decision record additionally requires:

- program name and legal entity; eligible jurisdictions, customer/account
  types, positions, and exclusions; default state and every enrollment path;
- whether the customer, provider, or borrower selects loans; whether an
  enrolled asset is guaranteed to lend; utilization, rate formation, gross
  borrower fee, customer share, provider/affiliate share, deductions, payment
  cadence, and a worked net-dollar example at a stated position and horizon;
- title, beneficial ownership, borrower/counterparty exposure, collateral
  amount/type/location/marking, insolvency treatment, SIPC or other protection
  boundaries, indemnity, and every disclosed loss scenario;
- dividends or payments in lieu, withholding and tax-document treatment,
  voting/proxy and corporate-action rights, sale/transfer/withdrawal behavior,
  recall timing, liquidity or close-out limits, and interaction with margin,
  orders, tax lots, and the strategy risk model;
- statement/API events, reconciliation identifiers, notices, rate and term
  changes, support and dispute paths, opt-out/revocation timing, residual
  obligations, and provider termination; and
- a receiver-facing explanation of expected net benefit, changed rights and
  risks, material unknowns, alternatives (including not enrolling), consent
  version/time, pause or revoke control, and post-action verification.

Rates, demand, loan allocation, collateral marks, tax outcomes, and recall
behavior are time-varying observations, not permanent product capabilities.
Separate the program's contractual rule from the account's current state and
the simulation's model. When only paper/sandbox access exists, the MEET result
for lending stays `source-only` unless that environment demonstrably exercises
the program; add every unmodeled item from the omission ledger to the live or
customer acceptance assay. Never recommend enrollment from yield copy alone,
and never treat an account-wide opt-in as authority to lend a particular
customer position without the applicable mandate and disclosures.

## Spec-stage application (deploy is not the gate)

This discipline applies at spec time, not only at ship time. A surface
that is being specced — an endpoint shape, a manifest format, an API
contract documented but not yet deployed — still runs the full
procedure and records its output *in the spec itself*: bounded arenas and
receiver, classified incumbent portfolio, measured capability surface, MEET
contract, and BEAT hypothesis with the step-5 falsification pass already done. "We'll do
meet-then-beat when we build it" is the failure mode this section exists
to close — a spec written without it ships blind, either duplicating an
incumbent feature under a new name (nothing to beat) or over-claiming a
differentiator that a five-minute check would have found incumbents
already ship. A complete spec is evidence that the competitive procedure ran;
it is not evidence that a MEET gap closed or a BEAT advantage shipped. It must
name the receiver-visible wedge and its acceptance assay as the next roadmap
item. Re-run step 3's measurement before the surface actually deploys, since a
spec can go stale against incumbents that shipped in the interim.

Any owner of a public property; the ops.561.group control plane; the
education platform; any enterprise section whose offer names a market
