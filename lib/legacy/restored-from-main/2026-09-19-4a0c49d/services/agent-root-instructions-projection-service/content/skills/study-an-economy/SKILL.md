# Study an economy

The founder's order of 2026-09-05: a deeper study of the participation economy,
how to define it, and how to minmax it, resource by resource -- "we have way
more economics theory than I think we're using to handle our operations."

writing anything, and writes rows before prose, per the founder's own method.

## The order of work

1. **Rows first.** Every currency, credit, commodity, or scarce input the
   economy under study touches gets one resource row against the schema
   above before any prose is written about it. Unknown fields are written
   `"unknown"`, never guessed.
2. **Taxonomy axes, not only names.** A taxonomy claim ("five American
   economics", or any other grouping) states the axes that separate its
   members, not merely their labels.
3. **Minmax as a sizing problem.** The minmax is measured as position
   sizing under a labor budget with risk of ruin (sizing-without-a-bank),
   never as growth-optimal Kelly sizing, which assumes an outside bank that
   replays every ruined trajectory.
4. **Prose last.** Narrative synthesis is written only after the rows and
   the taxonomy axes exist, and cites them rather than restating them.
