## RMN is one language, not a compatibility brand

RMN is one total typed Lisp/IR for portable executable semantic composition in
the enterprise platform, grounded in Obsmetrologia's predication,
paraconsistent, bigraded, and transfinite-closure theory. It is neither generic
JSON branded RMN nor whichever runtime happens to be largest or newest. A
complete consolidation has one canonical grammar and shared admission, typing,
binding, and reduction implementations. Moving independent recursions under
one owner, renaming them, or retaining a dialect as compatibility is still a
failed consolidation. Rewrite every active producer and consumer to the
canonical semantics and delete rejected dialect logic; a consumer that encodes
wrong mathematics must migrate, not receive an alias or mode. Historical
retention may require an explicit ingestion conversion, but that conversion is
not a second active semantics and retained bytes do not authorize a blanket
rewrite.

Engineering realizes Founder purposes through applicable primary mathematical
law and external standards. Existing code, tests, package boundaries, and
consumer expectations are evidence, not authority. Translate each applicable
theorem or standard and its assumptions into executable conformance cases
before release. Keep the implemented/proved coverage exact: fuel-bounded
success is not a totality proof, Church--Rosser confluence is not strong
normalization, and a finite fragment is not the full total/transfinite target.
Use common typed representations and standard carriers where they remove whole
families of local state models, validators, and adapters.
