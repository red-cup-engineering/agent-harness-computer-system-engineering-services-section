## Layer 0: The Substrate

Data, Code, and Networking components represent the foundational substrate of our software ecosystem.

Data is the most primitive, with Code operationalizing it, and Networking transmitting it.

The three together provide the required components of the systems.

At present, this is our messiest and least-standard part of the codebase, because it's trying to provide the tooling for *everything* else, and everything else is too unstable to have consistent requirements.

In this note, I'll point to families of capabilities/codecs with *-stuff, to reference whatever package(s) hold the relevant substrate-stuff in computer-[data|code|networking]-engineering

We have a few "special" elements of our substrate, that operate over generic stuff, to provide our ecosystem with the capabilities we need

### semiotic-content-address

This is meant to be our weird NI that integrates in proof-pathed-addressing concepts. (Developing clear specifications for these formats and protocols is a critical thing to do)

### semiotic-expression-notation

This is meant to be a specialized JSOn that is a total typed LISP built from Obsmetrologia's mathematics.

I think it's currently called relational-model-notation, but we want to settle on "semiotic-" as the prefix for our "house" formats and protocols.

Semiotic Expression Notation is meant to carry Semiotic Content Addresses as its terms.

Right now, we probably have ooodles of implementations of relational-model-notation, and critically, past agents wrote two versions of the language, one with more opcodes, that violated the rules of the underlying mathematics. We need truly just the one notation system, LISP-y carried over JSON.

This is meant to be EAVT, but where each E, A, and V, is a SEN.

Obs = observation|obstruction
Metrologia: metrology

Obsmetrologia provides many logical paradigms for understanding our systems.

### Sign-Stuff

It's my intention that each facet of the substrate (data, code, networking) be realized as modules|specializations of what I've come to call our "Sign-Stuff" packages:

- SignCell, which is mean to provide the data structure and methods that alll computational data can share, informed by our deep mathematical research into computational logics
- SignChain, which is meant to provide the logic structures and methods that all computational code can share.
- SignGraph, which is meant to provide the graphing structures and methods that SignCells and Chains form

There's a fourth Sign package, SignCraft, which is meant to be a part of Layer 1

#### SignCell
- requires: metrologic

SignCell has previously been called Semiotic Cell, Closure Cell, and various other names. Our goal is to get allll of those variants condensed into one solid application offering.

#### SignChain
- requires: signcell, metrologic

SignChain is, mathematically speaking, the bilattice of knowledge order and truth order, that form evidence. SignChain is what has previously been framed as a proofing engine, or memetic processing unit.

#### SignGraph
- requires: signcell, signchain, metrologic

SignGraph is the higher-order categorical stuff above SignChain.
